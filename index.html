ABOUT THE GAME

A JOURNEY THROUGH A WORLD OF MYSTERY

Prism's Illusion is an action RPG experience set in a fantastical world shaped by mystery, conflict, and the power of light. Explore striking environments, face powerful adversaries, and uncover the secrets that lie beyond what the eye can see.

As the journey unfolds, players are invited to discover the world and piece together the truth behind its strange and beautiful illusions.

THE LORE

BEYOND THE FRACTURED LIGHT

Light can reveal a truth—or conceal it. Within the world of Prism's Illusion, the line between reality and illusion is a mystery waiting to be explored.

Every place holds traces of a greater story, and every encounter may bring the world’s hidden history into focus. The deeper the journey goes, the more questions emerge about what is real, what has been lost, and what lies ahead.
